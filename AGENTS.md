# Repository Instructions

This repository is for preparing a competition presentation deck for the 2026 HUSS Social AI Lab competition.

## Required Context

Before planning, writing, or editing presentation content, read and use:

- `docs/competition-brief.md`

Treat that brief as the source of truth for the competition's purpose, eligibility, process, judging criteria, submission constraints, and presentation strategy.

## Presentation Priorities

When creating or revising deck content, emphasize:

- A clear public-demand problem backed by a real requesting institution
- Connection to the HUSS consortium theme
- AI usage that is appropriate, explainable, and documented
- Humanities and social-science considerations: policy, ethics, law, governance, social acceptance, and user experience
- Concrete implementation and field-application plan
- Evidence such as institution contact records, meeting notes, data sources, AI prompts, and AI usage details

## Constraints To Preserve

- Final presentation should fit the competition requirement of 10-20 PPT pages.
- The core pitch should be optimized for a 5-minute team presentation.
- DeepSeek must not be used as an LLM for this competition.
- AI usage and related prompts must be documented.
- Do not treat the project as a generic AI product pitch; frame it as a public-problem solution with social acceptability and real-world execution.
